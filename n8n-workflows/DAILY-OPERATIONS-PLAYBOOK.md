### **🌆 AFTERNOON DEEP WORK (Only on specific days)**

#### **TUESDAY/THURSDAY: Discovery Calls (2:00 - 4:00 PM)**
**15-30 minutes per call**

**5-Minute Pre-Call Prep:**
1. Review company website
2. Check their recent campaigns
3. Open 3 relevant case studies

**Call Script (15 mins):**
1. **(2m)** Intro: "We create brand films & content for EU companies"
2. **(5m)** Discover: "What do you need? Timeline? Budget?"
3. **(5m)** Pitch: Show 2 case studies
4. **(3m)** Close: "I'll send proposal by Thursday"

**Post-Call (5 mins):**
- Update sheet: Lead Status = "Qualified"
- Add to proposal queue
- Send thank-you email

---

#### **THURSDAY: Proposal Writing (3:00 - 5:00 PM)**
**30-45 minutes per proposal**

**Simple Proposal Structure:**
1. What they need (1 paragraph)
2. What we'll deliver (bullet points)
3. Timeline (2-4 weeks)
4. Pricing (€6-15K based on scope)
5. **Payment Terms:**
   - 💵 50% upfront (€3-7.5K) - covers pre-production + travel
   - 💵 50% before final delivery
   - ➡️ 2 revision rounds included
6. Portfolio links (3 case studies)
7. Next step: "Sign & pay 50% to start"

**After sending:**
- Mark in spreadsheet: Proposal sent date
- Set reminder: Follow up in 3 days

---

### **🌃 EVENING WRAP: 5:00 - 5:10 PM (10 mins)**

**Final check:**
- [ ] Any contracts signed today?
- [ ] Any deposits received?
- [ ] Update column AL: "Deposit Paid" if yes
- [ ] Tomorrow: Any calls? Block calendar

**✅ DONE FOR THE DAY!**
