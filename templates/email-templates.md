## 📧 EMAIL 1: Initial Outreach (Day 0)

### **Subject Line Options (A/B Test These):**

**Option A (Specific Reference):**
```

```

**Option B (Direct Value):**
```

```

**Option C (Curiosity):**

```

I'm reaching out because we specialize in high-impact audiovisual production for European enterprises. We've worked with companies like {{similar_company_1}} and {{similar_company_2}} to create:

• Brand films & corporate storytelling
• Product launch campaigns

• Executive & team features

Would you be open to a brief conversation about how we could support {{Company}}'s 2026 content strategy?

Best regards,
{{Your_Name}}
Masala Studios

P.S. - Here's a recent project we did for {{similar_industry}}: {{portfolio_link}}
```

---

### **Personalization Variables:**
- `{{Company}}` - Company name (e.g., "Siemens")
- `{{FirstName}}` - Contact first name (e.g., "Christiane")
- `{{specific_campaign}}` - Recent initiative (check LinkedIn/website)
- `{{specific_compliment}}` - Genuine praise ("visual storytelling", "production quality")
- `{{similar_company_1/2}}` - Name-drop relevant clients
- `{{their_country}}` - Their location ("Germany", "France")
- `{{similar_industry}}` - Match their sector
- `{{portfolio_link}}` - Relevant case study URL

## 📧 EMAIL 3: Breakup Email (Day 14)

### **Subject Line:**
```
Closing the loop - {{Company}}
```

---

### **Email Body:**

```
Hi {{FirstName}},

I'll make this my last note (promise!).

**If now's not the right time:** No worries at all—I'll reach back out in Q3 when planning cycles typically ramp up.

**If there IS interest:** Just hit reply and I'll send over:
• Full portfolio (15+ case studies)
• Pricing breakdown by project type
• 3 references from {{their_industry}} clients
• Available production dates for Q2/Q3

Either way, wishing you and the {{Company}} team continued success with your 2026 campaigns.

Best,
{{Your_Name}}
Masala Studios

---

P.S. - If you know someone else at {{Company}} who handles video production, feel free to forward this along. Always happy to connect!
```

---

### **Why This Works:**
1. **Permission-based close** - "my last note" builds goodwill
2. **Two clear paths** - Easy yes/no decision
3. **Reduces friction** - Specific deliverables if they reply
4. **Referral ask** - Opens door to other contacts
5. **Leaves door open** - "Q3" gives them out without guilt

## 🎯 **Template Usage Guidelines:**

### **DO:**
- ✅ Personalize EVERY email (company research)
- ✅ Reference specific campaigns/initiatives  
- ✅ Name-drop similar companies (builds credibility)
- ✅ Include Calendly link (makes booking easy)
- ✅ Keep under 150 words (respect their time)
- ✅ Use bullet points (easy scanning)

### **DON'T:**
- ❌ Send generic mass emails
- ❌ Use ALL CAPS or excessive punctuation!!!
- ❌ Attach large files in first email
- ❌ Be pushy or aggressive
- ❌ Talk only about yourself
- ❌ Send follow-ups before 7 days
